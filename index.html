VantaGe Publisher

VantaGe Publisher is the brand's internal tool. It runs on the brand owner's own computer and uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the brand's own finished videos to the brand's own YouTube channels - each video goes up as private with a scheduled publication time, and only after the owner has confirmed that particular upload by typing a confirmation in the terminal;
  • check that the signed-in Google account is the channel the video is meant for, before anything is uploaded;
  • read YouTube Analytics for the brand's own videos (views, engaged views, average view duration and percentage, likes, subscribers gained) so the owner can compare how the videos perform and make the next ones better.

The tool is not offered to the public. There are no user accounts and no sign-up: the only person who signs in to it with Google is the brand owner, with the Google account that owns the VantaGe channels.

How the tool handles data is described in the Privacy Policy; the rules for using it are in the Terms of Service.
